### Thank you for participating! This study examines how readers use real-world knowledge and story context to understand language. Some events are unlikely in everyday life but could happen in a dream. We are interested in whether readers continue to rely on real-world knowledge when reading about dreams, or adjust their expectations to fit the dream context. During the experiment, you read sentences describing plausible or implausible events in either realistic or dream contexts. We measured how long you spent reading each word. Longer reading times can indicate that information requires more processing. The comprehension questions helped us check that participants were paying attention to the sentences. This project replicates Experiment 2 of Jiang and Filik (2025). We are testing whether implausible events take longer to read in both contexts, or whether a dream context reduces—or even reverses—this difference. Replicating the study helps us assess how consistently its findings hold in a new sample of participants. If you have questions about this study, please contact Lumi Kang at l5kang@ucsd.edu.

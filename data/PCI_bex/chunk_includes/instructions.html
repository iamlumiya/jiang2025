Welcome to the Experiment!

Please read the following instructions carefully before you start the experiment. Thank you!

Please use a laptop or a desktop computer instead of a tablet or a phone for this experiment. Before you start, please put your phone and other electronic devices on mute; please make sure that you are seated alone in a quiet room, and that you won't be disturbed by other people in the next 40 minutes.

Your task in this experiment is to read sentences from the computer screen. They are displayed to you word by word. First, an asterisk (*) appears in the middle of the monitor. When you are ready, please press the SPACEBAR to start reading a new sentence.

Then you will see a sentence. At first, you will only see underlines indicating how long this sentence will be:

_____ _____ _______ __ ___ ____ ____

To see the first part of the sentence, please press the SPACEBAR:

Roger _____ _______ __ ___ ____ ____

As soon as you press the SPACEBAR again, the next part appears and the previous one disappears again, etc.

_____ likes _______ __ ___ ____ ____
_____ _____ reading __ ___ ____ ____

Please keep one finger on the SPACEBAR at all times while reading. Once you have read each word, please press the SPACEBAR to reveal the next word in the sentence. Read the sentences at your own normal reading speed so that you understand the content.

Some sentences are followed by a short statement. Please judge whether this statement is correct based on the previously read sentence by pressing S or L.

Once you have given an answer, you cannot revise it. Just move on to the next sentence. A comprehension question looks like this:

Roger does not like reading.
S = Right              L = Wrong

First you will practice the task with some example sentences. Then you will start the actual experiment, during which there will be a short break. If you have any questions about the tasks, please contact the experimenter:
